Sum Swipe – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Who we are

Sum Swipe is a mobile puzzle game made by RANDEV (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to us at randevloper@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

None. Sum Swipe has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run our own servers that receive information from the game.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score and your sound setting on your device only. This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sum Swipe shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location;
  • device and app information, such as device model and operating system version;
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example ad views and clicks);
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

This information is handled by Google under its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Consent in Europe and the UK. Where the law requires it, the game shows a consent message from Google before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the “Privacy options” link on the game’s main menu, which appears where it is required.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

6. Children

Sum Swipe is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

7. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell personal information and we do not share information with anyone, because we do not collect any ourselves. Data collected by Google through its advertising service is described in section 4.

8. Retention and deletion

We do not store personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google is kept according to Google’s policies. You can contact us with any request or question and we will respond.

9. Security

The game does not transmit your information to us. Data sent to Google by its advertising service is encrypted in transit.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
